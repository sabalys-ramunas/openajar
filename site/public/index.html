A computer of its own for your AI agent

Your AI helper (Claude Code, Codex and others) gets a real computer to work on, with a web browser and your project. You can watch its screen live, and step in with one click.

Install on a Mac or Linux, in one line
$ curl -fsSL https://openajar.com/install.sh | sh

It walks you through setup in about five minutes. Nothing costs money until your agent opens a computer.

You ask. It works on its own computer. You get the result.

What its computer has

Everything it needs to finish the job, not just talk about it.

Instead of saying "this should work", your agent opens the page and checks.

  • A real web browser

    It opens your website or app, clicks through it and takes a picture of what it sees, like a person checking their work.

  • A terminal

    It installs, builds and runs the tests there. Nothing runs on your laptop, and nothing clutters it.

  • Your project

    Your code is copied onto it. The agent's changes come back as a new branch with a pull request, for you to look at first.

  • A desktop

    Programs that are not web pages too, used with a real pointer and keyboard, while you watch.

  • It is still there tomorrow

    When it goes quiet, the whole computer is saved and switched off. Say "continue" in any new chat and it comes back as it was.

  • Sign-ins, without the password

    It can stay signed in to the accounts you choose. openajar types the login in for it, and the AI never sees the password.

Safe by default

Nothing gets looser without you.

The agent gets its own computer, not yours. Your keys, your main branch and your money stay protected, and only you can change the rules.

  • Only you loosen the rules

    Its limits and the logins it may use change only from your own computer, never from the agent's. Keeping a machine switched on all the time needs your yes first.

  • No keys on its computer

    Your passwords, GitHub and cloud keys stay on your laptop. A login you choose is typed in for it, and the AI never sees it.

  • Your work is never overwritten

    It pushes new branches for you to review. It cannot rewrite or delete your main branch, tags or anyone else's work.

  • Spending has a hard limit

    10 EUR a month, 4 hours per computer and 2 at once, unless you choose other limits. Past them, nothing opens.

  • Nothing left running

    An idle computer is saved and switched off after an hour, and unsaved work gets a rescue copy instead of being lost.

  • You can always look

    Every computer has a live view, and every command it ran is kept. Its commands never run on your laptop.

Questions

Good to know

Is openajar another AI?

No. It is the computer your AI works on. Your agent (Claude Code, Codex or another) keeps running where it runs today, and does its work on a computer openajar gives it.

What do I need?

A Mac or a Linux computer with git, an agent such as Claude Code or Codex, and a place for its computers: a Hetzner Cloud account (about a cent an hour while one works) or Docker on your own computer, for free.

Is my laptop safe?

The agent's commands run on its own computer, never on your laptop, and that computer holds none of your keys. Pushes go through openajar with a key for that one project, which ends with the computer.

Does my laptop need to stay on?

While an agent works, yes: the agent runs on your laptop and reaches its computer through openajar there. When the laptop sleeps, each computer is saved and switched off, so nothing bills overnight. For agents that work while you sleep, openajar can run on a small server.

Where does the name come from?

A door left ajar: the agent works in its own room, and the door is open for you to look in and step in. It was called agentos until November 2026, and setups from then keep working.
