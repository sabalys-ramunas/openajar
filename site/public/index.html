How a task goes

One evening, one task. The computer is still there in the morning.

Your agent stays where it is today, in the chat you already use. openajar only gives it a place to work, so your own laptop stays out of it.

  1. 22:10

    You ask, in plain words

    You, in Claude CodeWork in openajar on our shop: fix the failing checkout test, show me the page working, and open a pull request.

  2. 22:12

    It gets a computer of its own

    A machine in your own cloud account (or in Docker on yours), with your project already on it. Ready in about two minutes.

    A real browser A terminal A desktop Your project
  3. 22:20

    It checks its work, like a person would

    It runs the tests, starts the app and opens the page in its own browser, instead of saying it should work. Watch its screen live from a link, and take over with one click.

  4. 22:41

    You get proof, and a change to approve

    A screenshot of the page working, and the fix on a new branch with a pull request. Nothing reaches your main branch until you say so.

  5. 23:41

    Its computer goes to sleep

    After an hour with nothing to do, the whole machine is saved and switched off. A saved computer costs about 0.05 EUR a month.

  6. 08:30 · the next morning

    Say "continue", and it is back

    Same files, same branch, same browser still signed in, plus the note it left for itself. Back in about a minute.

Safe by default

Nothing gets looser without you.

The agent gets its own computer, not yours. Your keys, your main branch and your money stay protected, and only you can change the rules.

  • No keys on its computer

    Your passwords, GitHub and cloud keys stay on your laptop. A login you choose is typed in for it, and the AI never sees it.

  • Your work is never overwritten

    It pushes new branches for you to review. It cannot rewrite or delete your main branch, tags or anyone else's work.

  • Only you loosen the rules

    Its limits and the logins it may use change only from your own computer, never from the agent's. Keeping a machine on all the time needs your yes first.

  • Spending has a hard limit

    10 EUR a month, 4 hours per computer and 2 at once, unless you choose other limits. Past them, nothing opens.

  • Nothing left running

    An idle computer is saved and switched off after an hour, and unsaved work gets a rescue copy instead of being lost.

  • You can always look

    Every computer has a live view, and every command it ran is kept. Its commands never run on your laptop.

What it costs

openajar is free. Its computers cost cents.

You pay the cloud provider directly, from your own account (Hetzner Cloud or Morph Cloud), or nothing at all with Docker on your own computer.

Install

Ready in five minutes.

Nothing bills until an agent opens a computer, and setup puts a monthly cap on it.

  1. Paste one line in a terminal

    On a Mac or Linux. It installs the latest release and starts setup.

    $ curl -fsSL https://openajar.com/install.sh | sh
  2. Answer a few questions

    Where its computers run (a Hetzner Cloud token, or Docker for free), your GitHub login, and it connects Claude Code and Codex for you. Every step can be skipped.

  3. Ask your agent

    Work in openajar on github.com/you/app: fix the failing checkout test, show me the page working, and open a pull request.

Questions

Good to know.

Is openajar another AI?

No. It is the computer your AI works on. Your agent (Claude Code, Codex or another) keeps running where it runs today, and does its work on a computer openajar gives it.

What do I need?

A Mac or a Linux computer with git, an agent such as Claude Code or Codex, and a place for its computers: a Hetzner Cloud account (about a cent an hour while one works) or Docker on your own computer, for free.

Is my laptop safe?

The agent's commands run on its own computer, never on your laptop, and that computer holds none of your keys. Pushes go through openajar with a key for that one project, which ends with the computer.

Does my laptop need to stay on?

While an agent works, yes: the agent runs on your laptop and reaches its computer through openajar there. When the laptop sleeps, each computer is saved and switched off, so nothing bills overnight. For agents that work while you sleep, openajar can run on a small server.

Where does the name come from?

A door left ajar: the agent works in its own room, and the door is open for you to look in and step in. It was called agentos until November 2026, and setups from then keep working.
